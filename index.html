What is Big H?

Big H is a fast 1v1 football game for iPhone. Run, jump, head the ball and score more goals than the other player in quick two-minute matches. When your meter is full, press BIG H: your head grows huge for a few seconds.

  • Quick Match: play against a computer player.
  • World Tour: 8 stages and 24 matches with a wild twist on every stage.
  • Training: short lessons for every move, plus free play.
  • Local Duel: two players on one phone.

Big H is free. This version plays fully offline, with no account, no ads and no in-app purchases.

FAQ

How do I play?

Hold your iPhone sideways. Use ◀ and ▶ to run and ▲ to jump. Near the ball, press KICK for a low, hard shot or LOB to float the ball high. When the BIG H button lights up, press it for your power move. Training teaches every move step by step.

Can I move or resize the buttons?

Yes. Open Settings > Controls to change the button size and opacity, drag the buttons where you like (Edit controls layout), or switch to left-handed controls.

How do two people play on one phone?

Choose Local Duel. Each player picks a hero, then uses the buttons on their own half of the screen.

I can't hear anything.

Sound starts after your first tap on the screen. Turn the volume up with the side buttons. Big H plays sound even when the silent switch is on, and it mixes with your own music. Settings > Audio has separate volumes for sound effects, music and the crowd.

The game feels slow or my phone gets warm.

Open Settings > Graphics and pick Battery Saver. Auto chooses the best look your phone can run smoothly.

Too much flashing or shaking?

Open Settings > Accessibility and turn on Reduce flashes and Reduce motion. Larger text is there too.

Do I need an account? What do you know about me?

No account and no sign-in. Big H does not collect any personal data. Your progress, settings and the name you choose are saved only on your phone, and the game does not send them to us. See the privacy policy.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

This version has no cloud save, so progress stays on the phone where you play. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Can I play online?

Not in this version. Big H plays offline: against the computer, or with a friend on the same phone.
