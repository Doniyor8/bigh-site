Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25

The short version: Big H does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Who we are

Big H is an independent game ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Big H app for iPhone, version 1, which plays fully offline. If you have any question about it, write to doniyor@163.com.

Information we collect

None. This version of Big H does not connect to the internet or to any server of ours. It does not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send anything about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No ads. This version shows no advertising and contains no advertising code.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not measure how you play, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The app does not use the advertising identifier.
  • No purchases. There are no in-app purchases in this version.
  • No third-party code that collects data. The game is our own code, packed inside the app.

What stays on your device

To remember your game, Big H saves a few things in the app's own storage on your device: your progress (coins, unlocked heroes and items, World Tour stars, missions), your settings, the player name and flag you choose, a random player ID that the game makes on your device, and your age group. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it.

On the first screen the game asks for your year of birth. It keeps only the age group that year falls in (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over), not the year itself. The age group is used only on your device to set safe defaults (for example, younger players pick a name from a list instead of typing one). It is never sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes this saved data. Like other app data, it can be part of the backups your device makes (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Sharing and Photos

If you tap a Share button, the iPhone share sheet opens and you choose where the picture or video goes (for example Messages or Photos). We do not receive it. If you save to Photos, iOS asks for your permission first, and Big H only adds the file you chose to save; it never reads your photos.

Apple and the App Store

You download Big H from Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information about downloads and app use under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Apple. If you agree to share crash or usage information with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous reports about how the app is running.

Children's privacy

Because Big H does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Children see no ads and cannot buy anything in this version, and players under 13 pick their name from a list. If you are a parent and have a question, write to us.

Your choices and rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. You stay in control of the data on your device: you can change your name and settings in the game, Settings > Delete my data erases your saved game at once, and deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Changes to this policy

We plan to add online play, ads and in-app purchases in a later version. Before any such version is released, we will update this policy to explain exactly what changes, change the effective date at the top, and update the app's privacy details on the App Store.
